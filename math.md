Math + Science Club has three math tracks.
- **Group 1** is for 5--6th graders or students with little competition math experience. Recent topics include rates, divisibility, exponents, combinations and permutations, and quadratics.
- **Group 2** is for 7--8th graders. Recent topics include sequences and series, polynomial interpolation, states, mass points, and coordinate bashing.
- **Group 3** is for 9--10th graders and volunteers. Recent topics include cyclotomic polynomials, exponential generating functions and combinatorial classes, finite differences, orders modulo a prime, and complex bashing.
