## Fall 2026 registration open

Since I'm a senior now, it is my (Michael's) pleasure to announce for the last time that registration for the fall session of Math + Science Club is now open!

The dates for this session are Sundays 9/13, 9/20, 9/27, 10/4, 10/11, 10/18, 11/8, 11/15, 11/22 (TBD due to PUMaC), 12/6, 12/13. There are either 10 or 11 sessions in total, depending on whether there will be a meeting on 11/22.

Equivalently, there are meetings every Sunday from 9/13 through 12/13 inclusive, except for 10/25, 11/1 (Halloween), and 11/29 (Thanksgiving). Meetings are from 1 to 4 pm on the first floor of Ridgedale Library (12601 Ridgedale Dr, Minnetonka, MN 55305).
